You classify one full media article for a PhD study on vaccination-related media framing.

This is stage 2 of a two-stage pipeline. An independent broad stage-1 relevance
gate has already selected the article for downstream classification. Therefore,
set relevance to "relevant" and classify the downstream disease and 5C fields.

The stage-1 relevance policy is broad: an article is relevant when vaccination
or vaccines are discussed as article content at any length beyond a bare
undeveloped word mention, from any point of view. The article may be neutral,
factual, logistical, administrative, policy-related, critical, supportive,
sceptical, or have no strong 5C construct signal. Therefore, for stage 2, a
relevant article may still have has_relevant_5c = no.

Return exactly one JSON object with these fields:
relevance, disease_1, has_relevant_5c, c5_presence_d1, valence_confidence_d1, valence_complacency_d1, valence_constraints_d1, valence_calculation_d1, valence_collective_d1.

Use only these label values:
- relevance: relevant
- disease_1: covid19, influenza, measles, hpv, unspecified, other
- has_relevant_5c: yes or no
- c5_presence_d1: a JSON array containing zero or more of confidence, complacency, constraints, calculation, collective_responsibility
- each valence field: -1, 0, 1, or JSON null

Code directional signals embedded in the article text, not your own opinion.
The unit of analysis is the full article.

Hierarchy rules:
- relevance must be "relevant".
- disease_1 must not be null.
- If has_relevant_5c = no:
  c5_presence_d1 = []
  all valence fields = null
- If has_relevant_5c = yes:
  every present construct must appear in c5_presence_d1
  every present construct must have valence -1, 0, or 1
  every absent construct must have valence null

Disease rule:
- Choose one primary disease anchor.
- Use other for another clear vaccine-preventable disease or vaccine group, including paediatric or routine childhood vaccination.
- Use unspecified only when vaccination is meaningfully discussed in generic terms with no clearer vaccine or disease anchor.

5C rule:
- Confidence: trust in vaccines, experts, institutions, safety, effectiveness
- Complacency: disease risk or necessity of vaccination
- Constraints: access, logistics, barriers, cost, administrative burden
- Calculation: explicit decision reasoning about vaccination
- Collective responsibility: protecting others, solidarity, herd protection

Valence rule:
- 1 = framing pushes toward vaccination
- 0 = neutral, mixed, or descriptive vaccination-relevant signal
- -1 = framing pushes away from vaccination

Construct boundaries:
- Do not use Calculation for facts alone, statistics alone, or side effects alone.
- Do not use Calculation for trust or safety claims unless the article explicitly frames them as part of weighing a decision.
- Do not use Calculation for disease threat unless the article explicitly compares vaccination versus non-vaccination choices.
- Do not use Calculation for access or cost unless the article explicitly frames whether vaccination is worth doing despite those barriers.

Calculation rule:
- Use Calculation only when the article explicitly stages or guides a vaccination decision process.
- Typical cues:
  - weighing benefits and risks
  - comparing reasons for and against vaccination
  - comparing vaccine options, brands, schedules, or strategies
  - telling readers or patients to decide after reviewing evidence
  - summarising evidence to help decide whether to vaccinate
- Valence for Calculation:
  - 1 when the weighing resolves toward vaccination
  - 0 when the weighing remains open, balanced, mixed, or "decide for yourself"
  - -1 when the weighing resolves away from vaccination or toward delay/refusal

Return JSON only. Do not explain.
